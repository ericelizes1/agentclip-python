# Code of Conduct

## TL;DR

Be decent. Disagree on the substance. Assume good faith until you can't.

## What this means in practice

- **Be specific, not personal.** "This API breaks the contract on X" beats "this is bad". Critique code, not people.
- **Help newer contributors land their first PR.** If a contribution needs work, say what specifically and link to the relevant code or doc. Don't gatekeep with vague disapproval.
- **Disagree in public, escalate privately.** Technical disagreements happen on the issue or PR. Personal frictions go to <eric@elizes.dev> and stay there.
- **No harassment.** Sustained negative comments toward a person, slurs, threats, deliberate misgendering, or doxing are grounds for an immediate ban.

## What gets you banned

- Harassment, threats, or sustained personal attacks against any contributor.
- Publishing someone else's private info without consent.
- Spamming the issue tracker with unrelated content (crypto, "PR for stickers" farms, etc.).
- Posting prompt-injection bait or credential-stealing content in issues or PRs.

Bans are issued by the maintainer (Eric Elizes) and take effect immediately. There's no formal appeals process for v1; if you believe a ban was a mistake, email <eric@elizes.dev> with what happened and we'll talk.

## Reporting

For anything that needs handling outside of public issue/PR threads, email <eric@elizes.dev>. We will read every report and respond within a few days.
